# Foundation gap report

The work queue for the kind agents: what is missing from the platform foundation and the security-adjacent services, ranked. Everything up through a managed Kubernetes cluster is table stakes for a platform team establishing a target; security teams need what sits next to it; application primitives (Pub/Sub, Storage, Run, SQL, BigQuery) come after. The Cloud Foundation Fabric modules guide where effort goes (`catalog/docs/gcp-inventory.md`, v60.0.0).

Generated by `hack/coverage` from `hack/coverage/foundation.yaml` (the ranking and the judgement, by hand) and the coverage data (the numbers); do not edit. Read [README.md](README.md) first for what the numbers mean and how far to trust them.

What these numbers miss, for the foundation:

- **Kinds for operations that are not creates** (IAM policies and members, Shared VPC attachment, a project's metadata, Cloud NAT, routes of a router) are in no row's resource, but the kinds exist and their fields are counted against the parent they fill (`overrides.yaml`, `subresources`). Whole-resource gaps of that kind are in the queue below with `measured: n/a` where there is nothing to count against.
- **Alpha-only kinds count as official.** Several foundation kinds exist only as `v1alpha1`, which the charts refuse; they are listed under "Official but alpha-only".
- **Matching is by name.** Terraform-derived kinds that rename fields (Compute Engine, Kubernetes Engine) are undercounted by about 15% of their fields.

## How the queue is ranked

1. **Importance.** A: platform foundation (organization, folders, projects, organization policy, IAM at every level, tags, contacts, Shared VPC, VPC networking, DNS, KMS, log sinks and buckets, a managed GKE cluster and Fleet). B: security-adjacent (Security Command Center, VPC Service Controls, Cloud Asset, audit, Binary Authorization, Secret Manager, CA, Armor). C: the rest.
2. **Kind of gap.** A whole resource (or a whole level of one) with no kind first, then a kind that exists only as alpha, then fields missing from a kind that exists.
3. **Module need**: how many Cloud Foundation Fabric modules create the resource (`catalog/docs/gcp-inventory.md`), most first.
4. **Size**: S (a field or a parent on an existing kind, or one small new kind), M (one or a few new kinds, or tens of fields), L (a large kind or many).

**Route.** A *whole* or *stability* gap is built here, in our API group, by the copy-upstream rule (`CONTRIBUTING.md`). A *field* gap on an official kind cannot be fixed here without a parallel kind of our own; `docs/design.md` decision 5 says to wait for the release unless a field is critical. Each field item says what upstream is doing, and the ones that need the builder to decide are marked **Decision**.
